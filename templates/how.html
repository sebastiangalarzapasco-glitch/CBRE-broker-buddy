How Broker Buddy works

The model proposes. Code checks every fact against the transcript and the deal record. A broker approves anything that leaves.

LAYER 1 · CAPTURE

Voice in, text out

Dictation in the browser (Web Speech API), or typed notes. The broker checks the text before processing.

LAYER 2 · INTELLIGENCE

Claude reads the debrief

Claude Sonnet 5.5 (most careful) or Claude Haiku 5.5 (fastest) returns structured facts. Every value must come with the exact words it was taken from.

LAYER 3 · CHECKS AND DATA

One record per deal

Code verifies quotes, names and numbers, then updates the deal in SQLite, logs what changed and computes dates and cap rates.

LAYER 4 · EXPERIENCE

Review, then send

Today view, deal brief, team board and an email queue. Nothing is sent without a named approver.

Checks on every debrief

  • Every term, task and correction must quote the transcript word for word, or it is marked Needs review.
  • Names must exist in the transcript or the CRM. The model cannot invent a person, company or property.
  • Corrections and rejected proposals are kept, so they can be blocked from every email.
  • Dates are resolved from the meeting date. Deadlines and cap rates are calculated by code, not by the model.
  • Terms not discussed are recorded as Not mentioned instead of guessed.

Checks on every email

  • Every dollar amount must match the current deal record.
  • Corrected numbers, the other side's rejected proposals and internal estimates are blocked.
  • Any mention of wiring adds a fraud warning: verify instructions by phone.
  • No address on file means no draft. The broker is told instead.
  • Honest status: Draft, Approved, Sent or Failed. Never "Delivered" unless it really was.

Tested against an answer key

Four sample debriefs with hand-checked answers (Broker_Buddy_Answer_Key.xlsx). The automated tests run them on every change, plus tests that feed the checks deliberately wrong model answers.

Limitations

  • All deals, people and numbers are synthetic until CBRE provides masked data.
  • No live CRM, calendar or market data connection yet.
  • Dictation quality depends on the browser and the room.
  • Sample mode only knows the four sample debriefs; new ones need an API key.
